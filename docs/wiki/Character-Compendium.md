### Tier 3: Hamburglar (Hamilton Burglar)
- **Motivation:** Forgotten mascot, stole Great Recipe to be noticed
- **Encounters:** Ch.9 (indirect, captures Grimace), Ch.17 (emotional boss battle)
- **Arc:** Tragic villain → redeemed ally. Sacrifices position to help party.
- **Combat:** Overwhelming power initially, emotional mid-battle dialogue

### Tier 4: The McLord of Shadows
- **Nature:** Ancient entity sealed when Golden Arches were first raised
- **Goal:** Destroy Arches' magic, return everything to void
- **Final Boss:** 4-phase battle (shadow → Engine absorption → Shadow That Eats → Golden Arch Purification)
- **Defeat:** Sealed again (mercy, not destruction)
